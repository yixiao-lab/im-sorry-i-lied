# I'm Sorry I Lied — AI Halloween Costume Reveal

The costume you imagined. The homemade outfit you actually wore.

[I'm Sorry I Lied](https://imsorryilied.org/) is an AI video project designed to turn a childhood Halloween costume photo into an 8-second, vertical dream-versus-reality reveal.

Remember being a magical butterfly—with paper wings? Or a terrifying vampire in a very homemade cape? This project takes that contrast and turns it into a short, playful costume story.

**[Visit the project website →](https://imsorryilied.org/)**

## The idea

Start with the outfit you meant to be wearing. Then reveal the costume in your childhood photo.

The intended effect moves from an imagined, polished costume to a homemade interpretation inspired by the original picture. The humor comes from your own memory: what you thought you looked like versus what the photo remembers.

## Intended workflow

1. **Choose your photo.** Use a childhood costume photo of yourself or a photo of your own child, with one clearly visible face.
2. **Name the costume.** Tell the generator what the outfit was supposed to be—a butterfly, vampire, witch, or another costume.
3. **Create the reveal.** The planned flow combines an AI scene image with an 8-second costume reveal video.
4. **Download and share.** The account library is designed to let you view and download the finished clip. Add your own caption and sound in your preferred social app.

## Caption ideas

These are writing prompts to add when posting, not captions embedded in the generated video:

> “I said I was a butterfly. I was wearing paper wings.”

> “I remember a terrifying vampire. My parents remember a towel cape.”

> “The costume in my head had a much bigger budget.”

## A few things to know

- AI results can change facial details, clothing, and the background. Review the result before sharing.
- The effect is a creative interpretation, not a guaranteed recreation of your original photo or a specific viral video.
- The generated video does not include TikTok's trend sound or on-screen captions.
- I'm Sorry I Lied is an independent project, not affiliated with TikTok or the original sound creator.
